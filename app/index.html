How a deal earns its badge

  • NEW LOW — the named source called it the product's lowest price ever. Only 13 of 30 deals earn it.
  • STRONG DEAL — 40% or more off the usual price, with the cut confirmed at the store.
  • GOOD DEAL — 20–39% off. A real saving, not a record.
  • FAIR DEAL — under 20% off. Worth buying if you needed it anyway, not worth rushing for.

Every price on this page was read on Nov 27, 2026 from the deals source named on its card, which names the store. Each discount is computed from the two prices shown, so the math always adds up.
